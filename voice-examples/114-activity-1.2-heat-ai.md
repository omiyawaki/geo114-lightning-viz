## Activity 1.2: Using AI to Analyze City Responses to Heat

### Learning Goal
Practice using AI as a research tool to quickly extract insights from policy documents.

### Setup
Form groups of 4-5 people. Each group will analyze one city's heat plan.
- Group 1: New York City PlaNYC 2023
- Group 2: Vancouver Climate Adaptation Strategy 2024-25
- Group 3: Sydney Adapting for Climate Change
- Group 4: London The London Plan 2021

Your group #: ______

### Task

#### Step 1: AI-assisted Research
- Upload your city's resilience plan PDF to an AI of your choice.
- Ask the AI to read through the PDF and identify what the city is doing to better prepare for and make the community more resilient to extreme heat.

#### Step 2: Sanity Checking AI with the Source Document
Sanity check the AI’s response by searching for keywords (i.e., Ctrl+F on PC or Cmd+F on Mac) in the PDF. Locate where in the PDF the AI found that information to make sure it didn’t hallucinate. Divide up the statements found by the AI in step 1 across each member in your group to complete this step efficiently. If you finish your assigned statements before others, help out other students sanity check the statements they were assigned.

Take notes on:
- What page/section you verified the AI’s statement
- Any AI errors or hallucinations you caught

#### Step 3: Cross-city Discussions
- Form new groups with at least one representative from each city.
- Each person shares their city's approach.
- Then discuss and answer the following questions:

What approaches were common across all cities?

What approaches differed across cities? Why?

What approaches do you think would work best for making Schenectady more heat resilient? Why?

Did any approach confuse you? Were there any approaches you are skeptical of?
