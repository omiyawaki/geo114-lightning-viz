## Part 1: Tutorial

We will introduce all the tools you will use in this lab to investigate the meteorology of winter weather.

1. We will begin with the Multi-Radar Multi-Sensor (MRMS) archive. MRMS stitches radar imagery across the US to form a single nationwide viewer. This will help us identify the spatial structure of precipitation.

https://mrms.nssl.noaa.gov/qvs/product_viewer/

2. The National Weather Service archives their surface analysis (which shows the location of low and high pressures, cold, warm, and stationary fronts.

https://www.wpc.ncep.noaa.gov/html/sfc-zoom.php

3. Plymouth State’s meteorology website shows the weather balloon network in the US. We will use this to identify the station ID of balloon launch locations that we want to see.

https://vortex.plymouth.edu/myowxp/upa/raobplt-a.html

4. The website below shows the balloon launch data (skew-T log-p diagram) in a visually cleaner way than Plymouth State:

https://rawinsonde.com/thunder_app/
